## UC-05

* **ID / Code:** UC-05
* **Use Case Name:** Prepare an invoice draft
* **Description:** Prepare and preview an editable one-off, recurring or corrective proposal without issuing an invoice or consuming a definitive number.
* **Primary Actor:** Operational User
* **Other Initiating Contexts:** [UC-04](UC-04.md) supplies approved initial proration; [UC-06](UC-06.md) and [UC-15](UC-15.md) include draft preparation with their authorised recurring or corrective proposals. These internal calls require no new staff session.
* **Supporting Actors:** None
* **Scope:** Turbine internal invoicing and collections application.
* **Level:** User Goal
* **Related requirements:** BR-04, BR-06; UR: UR-03, UR-04; FR: FR2, FR2.3, FR2.5, FR14.2, FR2.9, FR2.10, FR2.11; NFR-01, NFR-02, NFR-04, NFR-05, NFR-06.
* **Source:** E pp. 3-5, 16.; E pp. 3-5, 8-9, 14.; E pp. 5, 12, 15.
* **Stakeholders & Interests:**
  * *Customer and billing recipients:* receive a correct invoice through an eligible channel.
  * *Assigned staff:* prepare invoices and resolve delivery failures.
  * *Turbine finance staff:* preserve numbering, issued amounts and dispatch evidence.
* **Preconditions:** Direct initiation requires authentication, invoice-creation permission and customer scope. [UC-04](UC-04.md), [UC-06](UC-06.md) and [UC-15](UC-15.md) supply their authorised customer/proposal context. Applicable taxes, terms and catalogue defaults are available.
* **Postconditions:** The proposal is saved as an editable draft with its preview and validation outcome. It has no definitive series number or issued PDF; issuance and sending have separate results in [UC-19](UC-19.md) and [UC-20](UC-20.md).
* **Success Guarantee:** An authorised proposal becomes a recoverable, validated draft available for a separate issuance decision.
* **Minimal Guarantee:** Drafts consume no definitive number. Invalid or conflicting edits do not overwrite committed data or rewrite issued invoices.
* **Trigger:** Staff prepare an invoice, or [UC-04](UC-04.md), [UC-06](UC-06.md) or [UC-15](UC-15.md) supplies a billable proposal.
* **Main Success Scenario:**
1. The Operational User selects the customer and invoice type.
2. The system offers applicable customer, catalogue and form defaults.
3. The Operational User enters lines with descriptions, quantities, unit prices, optional discounts and selected taxes in one currency.
4. The system validates the mandatory tax data.
5. The system calculates decimal totals and due dates from the agreed terms.
6. The system checks for conflicting edits.
7. The system previews the proposal and its billing effects.
8. The Operational User confirms the draft.
9. The system saves the editable proposal without a definitive number.

* **Extensions:**
  * **1a.** Authorised workflow supplies a proposal:
    1. The system uses the approved proposal supplied by [UC-04](UC-04.md), [UC-06](UC-06.md) or [UC-15](UC-15.md) in place of manual entry.
    2. The system performs the same validation and preview checks.
    3. The system saves the draft under the caller authority without demanding a staff session.
  * **4a.** Missing mandatory tax data:
    1. The system identifies the missing fields.
    2. The system preserves the recoverable draft without making it eligible for issuance.
  * **5a.** Invalid amounts:
    1. The system identifies the invalid values.
    2. The system retains valid draft input without issuing.
  * **5b.** Unusual amount:
    1. The system shows the configured non-blocking deviation warning defined in FR2.5.2.
  * **5c.** Invalid currency:
    1. The system identifies the currency reason.
    2. The system preserves valid draft input without issuing.
  * **6a.** Conflicting change:
    1. The system rejects the stale edit without overwriting the committed version.
  * **9a.** Draft correction:
    1. The Operational User edits the unissued amount or other draft data.
    2. The flow returns to step 4.
  * **9b.** Draft deletion:
    1. The Operational User confirms deletion of the unissued draft.
    2. The system removes the draft without consuming a number.
  * **9c.** Staff request issuance of the current draft:
    1. The system invokes [UC-19](UC-19.md) for the current draft as the conditional issuance extension of preparation.
  * **9d.** Existing issued invoice selected for a financial change:
    1. The system routes the correction to [UC-15](UC-15.md) without editing the original.
  * **2a.** Catalogue or template later changes:
    1. The system leaves already-issued invoice data and PDFs unchanged.
