## UC-14

* **ID / Code:** UC-14
* **Use Case Name:** Inspect audit and automation health
* **Description:** Consult protected immutable audit evidence and actual automation outcomes without changing history or performing recovery.
* **Primary Actor:** Super Administrator
* **Other Initiating Contexts:** Automated processes record their own executions as internal system behaviour. This consultation does not start those processes.
* **Supporting Actors:** None
* **Scope:** Turbine internal invoicing and collections application.
* **Level:** Summary
* **Related requirements:** BR-01, BR-04, BR-06; UR: UR-14; FR: FR9.1, FR9.2; NFR-04, NFR-05, NFR-06.
* **Source:** E pp. 8-9, 12.
* **Stakeholders & Interests:**
  * *Super Administrator:* diagnose failures and recover a validated state.
  * *Turbine:* retain intact financial history and usable backups.
  * *Customer:* retain documents and scoped access after recovery.
* **Preconditions:** The actor is authenticated with Super Administrator protected-audit and global-health consultation authority.
* **Postconditions:** Consultation changes no historical evidence. Failed items and stages retain their actual status; backup creation and restoration have separate contracts in [UC-21](UC-21.md) and [UC-22](UC-22.md).
* **Success Guarantee:** The Super Administrator can inspect traceable audit and automation outcomes, including failed stages.
* **Minimal Guarantee:** Audit history cannot be edited or deleted. Inspection never declares a failed process or recovery complete.
* **Trigger:** The Super Administrator requests audit or automation-health inspection.
* **Main Success Scenario:**
1. The Super Administrator selects audit or health inspection.
2. The system displays permitted actions and executions with actor or process, target, time and outcome.
3. The Super Administrator filters the evidence.
4. The Super Administrator opens an event.
5. The system displays the event evidence and actual item status.
6. The system displays last recurring generation, pending dispatches, recent integration failures and last successful backup with actionable issue identifiers.

* **Extensions:**
  * **1a.** Protected consultation requested without Super Administrator authority:
    1. The system rejects global audit or backup-detail access without affecting separately scoped customer timelines.
  * **5a.** Audit modification requested:
    1. The system rejects mutation of the audit trail.
  * **5b.** Automated item failed:
    1. The system shows the failed item and stage without presenting the whole process as successful.
  * **6a.** Manual backup selected:
    1. The Super Administrator invokes [UC-21](UC-21.md) under backup authority.
  * **6b.** Recovery selected:
    1. The Super Administrator invokes [UC-22](UC-22.md) under recovery authority.
