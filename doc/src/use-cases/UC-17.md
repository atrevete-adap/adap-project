## UC-17

* **ID / Code:** UC-17
* **Use Case Name:** Decide a sensitive financial approval
* **Description:** Approve or reject the exact financial proposal under authority thresholds, substitution and separation-of-duties rules.
* **Primary Actor:** Administrator
* **Supporting Actors:** None
* **Scope:** Turbine internal invoicing and collections application.
* **Level:** User Goal
* **Related requirements:** BR-03, BR-04, BR-06; UR: UR-06, UR-07; FR: FR3.5, FR11, FR11.1, FR11.3; NFR-02, NFR-04, NFR-05, NFR-06.
* **Source:** E pp. 3, 11, 14.
* **Stakeholders & Interests:**
  * *Requester:* receive a decision on the submitted values.
  * *Administrator:* control financial exposure.
  * *Customer:* be charged only under approved exceptions.
* **Preconditions:** A current proposal identifies operation, values, reason and requester; the actor has its threshold authority. Higher thresholds require Super Administrator authority and any separation-of-duties rule still applies.
* **Postconditions:** The decision and exact proposal version are retained; only the approved current proposal can continue.
* **Success Guarantee:** Approval/rejection is recorded with actor/time/reason and routed to the originating workflow.
* **Minimal Guarantee:** A stale or rejected decision cannot activate changed terms or bypass a required second person.
* **Trigger:** An eligible approver opens a pending request.
* **Main Success Scenario:**
1. The system lists requests within the approver authority.
2. The Administrator reviews the exact customer proposal and its financial impact.
3. The system checks threshold authority.
4. The system checks separation of duties.
5. The system checks the current proposal version.
6. The Administrator records the approval decision with a comment.
7. The system stores the decision with actor, time and proposal version.
8. The system notifies the requester.
9. The system returns the decision to the originating [UC-04](UC-04.md), [UC-19](UC-19.md) or [UC-15](UC-15.md) workflow before the proposed action can take effect.

* **Extensions:**
  * **1a.** Approver absent:
    1. The authorised Administrator designates a substitute or reassigns the pending request.
    2. The system checks the substitute authority.
    3. The system preserves the reassignment history.
  * **3a.** Threshold exceeds approver authority:
    1. The system requires an eligible higher-authority approver, including Super Administrator authority where configured.
  * **4a.** Forbidden self-approval:
    1. The system rejects the decision pending an eligible second person.
  * **5a.** Proposal values changed:
    1. The system rejects the stale decision pending review of the current proposal.
  * **5b.** Prior decision already recorded:
    1. The system rejects the repeated decision without repeating the financial action.
  * **6a.** Proposal rejected:
    1. The Administrator records rejection with a comment.
    2. The system retains prior terms without activating the proposal.
    3. The system notifies the requester of the rejection.
  * **9a.** Uncollectible debt approved:
    1. The system records the approved write-off reason and uncollectible state distinctly from paid.
    2. The system retains financial evidence and the applicable collection decision.
