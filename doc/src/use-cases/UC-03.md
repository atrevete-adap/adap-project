## UC-03

* **ID / Code:** UC-03
* **Use Case Name:** Administer staff access and customer assignments
* **Description:** Administer internal accounts and customer portfolios with separate capabilities for account changes, assignment and temporary delegation.
* **Primary Actor:** Administrator
* **Other Initiating Contexts:** Super Administrator administers privileged accounts; each account/assignment branch retains its own authority check.
* **Supporting Actors:** Communication Service (account activation)
* **Scope:** Turbine internal invoicing and collections application.
* **Level:** User Goal
* **Related requirements:** BR-04; UR: UR-10, UR-12; FR: FR6.2, FR6.3, FR1.5, FR6.4, FR14.1; NFR-04, NFR-05.
* **Source:** E pp. 7-8, 12.; E p. 8.
* **Stakeholders & Interests:**
  * *Administrator:* allocate work and arrange temporary cover.
  * *Internal staff:* receive only authorised accounts and portfolios.
  * *Turbine:* retain responsibility for past actions when staff leave.
* **Preconditions:** The actor is authenticated with the capability for the selected operation. Account/role administration requires Super Administrator or explicitly delegated Administrator authority under FR6.3; assignment requires Administrator authority.
* **Postconditions:** Confirmed account or assignment changes are audited and enforced. Deactivation ends sessions; reassignment transfers pending work without rewriting historical ownership.
* **Success Guarantee:** The selected account, portfolio assignment or dated delegation is saved with its effective access scope.
* **Minimal Guarantee:** The actor cannot grant privileges beyond their authority; invalid requests confer no access and staff history is never deleted.
* **Trigger:** An authorised administrator selects an account or portfolio operation.
* **Main Success Scenario:**
1. The Administrator selects customers for assignment.
2. The Administrator chooses an eligible responsible staff member.
3. The system checks assignment authority and recipient status.
4. The system previews affected access and pending work.
5. The Administrator confirms the assignment.
6. The system saves the effective assignment.
7. The system routes pending work to the new responsible user.
8. The system records the change without altering historical attribution.

* **Extensions:**
  * **1a.** Account creation:
    1. The authorised account administrator enters a corporate email and a permitted role.
    2. The system validates domain, uniqueness and grant authority.
    3. The account administrator confirms creation.
    4. The system creates the account.
    5. The system requests activation delivery through Communication Service.
  * **1b.** Role change:
    1. The authorised account administrator selects the new role.
    2. The system checks grant authority.
    3. The account administrator confirms the change.
    4. The system saves and audits the permitted role.
  * **1c.** Account deactivation:
    1. The authorised account administrator confirms deactivation.
    2. The system revokes active sessions.
    3. The system flags pending assignments and approvals for reassignment.
    4. The system retains historical attribution.
  * **2a.** Temporary cover:
    1. The Administrator supplies delegation start and end dates.
    2. The system previews the dated access scope.
    3. The flow continues at step 3.
  * **3a.** Invalid delegation interval:
    1. The system rejects the delegation without granting access.
  * **3b.** Inactive recipient:
    1. The system rejects assignment without granting access.
  * **3c.** Privilege escalation requested:
    1. The system rejects the operation without granting access.
  * **5a.** Bulk reassignment:
    1. The system shows the affected count.
    2. The Administrator confirms the sensitive operation.
    3. The system rechecks each customer.
    4. The system reports individual successes and failures.
  * **6a.** Delegation expires:
    1. The system removes the temporary access automatically.
    2. The system retains the principal assignment and all prior actions.
  * **8a.** Activation message fails:
    1. The system records delivery failure separately from account creation.
