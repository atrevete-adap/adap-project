## UC-04

* **ID / Code:** UC-04
* **Use Case Name:** Manage subscriptions and annual rate reviews
* **Description:** Maintain monthly subscription terms and effective dates, including pauses, initial proration and an administrator-led annual rate review.
* **Primary Actor:** Operational User
* **Other Initiating Contexts:** An Administrator performs annual-rate decisions. Due-review task creation is internal system behaviour under the configured annual-review policy; it grants no approval authority.
* **Supporting Actors:** None
* **Scope:** Turbine internal invoicing and collections application.
* **Level:** User Goal
* **Related requirements:** BR-02, BR-03, BR-04; UR: UR-02, UR-03, UR-08; FR: FR2.2, FR5.2, FR10, FR10.1, FR10.2, FR5, FR5.1, FR5.3, FR5.4, FR5.5, FR10.3; NFR-01, NFR-04, NFR-05, NFR-06.
* **Source:** E pp. 2-5, 14.; E pp. 2-3, 11.
* **Stakeholders & Interests:**
  * *Assigned staff:* set up the service and follow up review tasks.
  * *Customer:* pay the approved price for the agreed service dates.
  * *Administrator:* validate annual revenue and exceptional commercial terms.
* **Preconditions:** The actor is authenticated and authorised for the customer; a rate table exists. Activation requires complete invoicing tax data. Annual rate activation requires Administrator approval authority.
* **Postconditions:** Approved terms, dates and review evidence are retained. Billing follows their effective dates; prior invoices and old debt remain intact.
* **Success Guarantee:** The subscription or approved future rate is stored with a traceable effective date and appropriate billing schedule.
* **Minimal Guarantee:** Unapproved terms are not billed, unavailable revenue is not invented and a pause never settles old debt.
* **Trigger:** Staff request a subscription operation, or the configured annual-review date becomes due and the system creates review tasks.
* **Main Success Scenario:**
1. The Operational User selects the customer, service, start date, rate, recurring concepts and agreed payment terms.
2. The system validates the required invoicing tax data.
3. The system previews the documented proration calculation and first regular billing date.
4. The Operational User reviews the proposal.
5. The Operational User confirms the authorised proposal.
6. The system stores the approved effective terms.
7. The system schedules monthly billing from the appropriate first day.
8. The system displays the subscription outcome.

* **Extensions:**
  * **2a.** Mandatory invoicing tax data missing:
    1. The system blocks activation with an explanation of the missing data.
  * **3a.** Future start:
    1. The system records pending activation until the planned date.
    2. The system generates no premature invoice or reminder.
  * **3b.** Approved start next month without proration:
    1. The system records the explicit no-proration option.
    2. The system schedules the first regular invoice without an extra overlapping charge.
  * **3c.** First-day start:
    1. The system schedules regular billing without an overlapping proration invoice.
  * **4a.** Discount requested:
    1. The system invokes [UC-17](UC-17.md) for the exact commercial exception.
    2. The system applies only approved effective dates.
    3. The system expires a time-limited discount automatically on its end date.
  * **4b.** Free period requested:
    1. The system invokes [UC-17](UC-17.md) for the proposed free period.
    2. The system applies only the approved dates.
  * **4c.** Extra service requested:
    1. The system invokes [UC-17](UC-17.md) for the agreed-price exception.
    2. The system applies only the approved service terms.
  * **1a.** Subscription pause:
    1. The Operational User records the reason and pause dates.
    2. The system suppresses the affected future monthly invoices without settling old debt.
    3. The system notifies the responsible user when reactivation becomes due.
  * **1b.** Subscription reactivation:
    1. The Operational User confirms the reactivation date.
    2. The system resumes eligible future billing from the effective date.
  * **1c.** Annual review due:
    1. The system lists customers due for review.
    2. The Administrator records annual revenue with its source and date, from manual entry or the simulated source.
    3. The system proposes the corresponding rate band.
    4. The system invokes [UC-17](UC-17.md) for the required human decision.
    5. The system retains the old and new values with the approver and approved effective date.
    6. The system activates the approved rate only from that date.
  * **1d.** Annual revenue evidence unavailable:
    1. The system leaves the review pending at the existing rate.
  * **1e.** Annual proposal rejected:
    1. The system retains the rejection at the existing rate.
  * **1f.** Annual review correction:
    1. The Administrator records corrected evidence with its reason.
    2. The system retains the correction history.
    3. The system invokes [UC-17](UC-17.md) before any explicit retroactive financial adjustment.
  * **8a.** Eligible mid-month start with proration:
    1. The system performs [UC-05](UC-05.md) with the approved initial prorated proposal as a conditional extension of this contract.
    2. The system invokes [UC-19](UC-19.md) to issue the resulting authorised draft.
    3. The system retains normal recurrence from the following first day.
  * **8b.** Prorated issuance fails:
    1. The system retains the subscription and the failed invoice stage separately.
    2. The system resumes [UC-19](UC-19.md) at the missing stage without duplicating the charge.
  * **8c.** Prorated delivery fails:
    1. The system retains the subscription and issued invoice.
    2. The system resumes [UC-20](UC-20.md) using the existing invoice and number.
